Withdrawal of the Rs. 5,000 Mess Caution Money Requirement

Respected Sir/Madam,

We, the students residing in the University Hostels, respectfully wish to draw your kind attention to Letter No. ACD45996/BBAU/26 dated 03/08/2026, particularly Point No. 5, which states:

Point No. 5

“Mess caution money of Rs. 5,000 shall be deposited by the hostel inmates at the time of allotment of hostel along with the hostel fee. Further, in case of recovery from caution money, the same has to be recouped immediately by the hostel inmates, to continue to avail the mess services.”

We would like to submit that many of us come from middle-class and financially modest backgrounds, and the additional payment of ₹5,000 places a considerable financial burden on us. As students of a public-funded university, we depend on affordable hostel and mess facilities to continue our education without undue financial hardship.

We, therefore, sincerely request the concerned authorities to kindly consider our concerns and withdraw the newly introduced requirement of ₹5,000 as mess caution money.

Furthermore, students who have already deposited the newly proposed amount under any circumstances, including admission cancellation, evacuation/vacation of hostels, or late submission of fees, should be immediately refunded the amount, irrespective of whether they are newly admitted students or already enrolled students.

We hope that our genuine concerns will be considered sympathetically and that a favourable decision will be taken in the interest of the students.

Thank you.

Yours sincerely,
